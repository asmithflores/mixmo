# Friendship shapes children’s predictions of emotion responses following conflicting outcomes

Authors anonymized for peer review.

## Study overview
Children aged 4–10 years were presented with scenarios in which two characters experienced conflicting outcomes (one winning and the other losing). Across trials, the relationship between the characters varied, allowing us to investigate how friendship shapes children's predictions of emotional responses.

This repository contains the open research materials associated with this study.
